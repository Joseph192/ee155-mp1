# Song Popularity Prediction

The assignment predicts a song's popularity category from the provided song data.

## Language

**Popularity category**:
The provided binary label for a song. The training file records it as `High` or `Low`; the assignment describes high as 1 and low as 0.
_Avoid_: Popularity score

**Audio feature**:
A provided measurement of a song's sound or structure, such as energy, danceability, or acousticness.
_Avoid_: Track identifier

**Track identifier**:
A Spotify URI or URL identifying a track, distinct from a measurement of its sound.
